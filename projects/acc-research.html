Adaptive Cruise Control Research

At Shiraz University's Dynamics and Control Lab I developed an adaptive cruise control strategy that considers road condition and lateral motion, not only the gap to the car ahead. It became my BSc thesis and led to two peer-reviewed papers on ADAS, motion planning and control.

How it works

Road-aware gap

Following distance and braking force adapt to road surface and weather, so the car keeps a longer gap on wet or icy roads.

Gentler following

A new tail-following method and throttle controller cut maximum deceleration by about 40% on average and reduce brake use.

Comfort in corners

Lateral motion is part of the comfort objective, which also produced a 10% gain in fuel efficiency.
