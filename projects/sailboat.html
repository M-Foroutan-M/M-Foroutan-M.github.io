Autonomous Sailboat

A model sailboat that sails itself to GPS waypoints. An Arduino reads GPS, compass heading and wind direction, works out the bearing and the wind angle relative to the boat, and adjusts rudder and sail in a closed loop. Every run is logged to an SD card for analysis.

/how

How it works

Sense

GPS position, CMPS12 compass heading and Davis wind direction and speed sensors are read every loop.

Decide

The controller computes the bearing and distance to the next waypoint and the relative wind angle.

Act

Servos set rudder angle to hold course and sail angle to suit the wind, closing the loop.

Log

Position, heading, bearing, wind and actuator positions are written to SD card, then plotted after each run.
