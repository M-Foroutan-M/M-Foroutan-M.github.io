360° Multi-Camera Platform

For my MSc dissertation (Best Dissertation Award) I designed a roof-mounted ring of eight fisheye cameras and four NVIDIA Jetsons that rides on the Jackal and records synchronised 360° video for social robot navigation benchmarking. Alongside it I built a ROS 2 pipeline that finds people in 360° images and estimates where they are and which way they face.

How it works

Camera ring hardware

Eight ELP fisheye cameras on a 3D-printed ring, a Jetson AGX Orin and Xavier NX units, a network switch and a 48 → 12 → 5 V power chain, all carried by the robot.

Synchronised capture

Every camera is time-aligned to within 2 ms, so frames from all eight views can be stitched and compared with LiDAR and odometry.

One click, full dataset

An 11-step pipeline links the RViz goal, Nav2 execution, SSH triggering of the Jetsons and rosbag2, so a whole recording session starts from one goal.

Finding people in 360°

Equirectangular frames from an Insta360 are reprojected into perspective views, YOLO detects people, and each person gets a ground-plane position, body orientation and a stable track ID.
