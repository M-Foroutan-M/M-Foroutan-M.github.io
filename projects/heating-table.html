Closed-Loop Heating Table

A MATLAB/Simulink model of a heated table sensed by a 6×8 grid and controlled zone by zone. Each zone runs its own PID loop with adaptive gain scheduling, and a shared sensing layout cut the number of sensor interconnections by 70%.

Coursework project. Simulink files available on request.

/how

How it works

Spatial sensing

Temperature is measured on a 6×8 grid so hot and cold spots across the surface are visible, not just one average.

Distributed control

The surface is split into zones, each with its own PID loop, so a disturbance in one corner is corrected locally.

Gain scheduling

Controller gains adapt with operating temperature to keep the response fast without overshoot across the range.

Simpler wiring

A shared sensing layout cut sensor interconnections by 70% compared with wiring every point individually.
